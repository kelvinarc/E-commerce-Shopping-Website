About Mainstreet Mall

We started with a simple idea: shopping online should be easy, honest and affordable for everyone.

Our story

Mainstreet Mall began as a small shop in Port Harcourt. Customers kept asking for delivery, so we built an online store that brings the whole shopping center to your phone or computer.

Today we work with trusted suppliers and local sellers to offer fashion, electronics, home goods and groceries at fair prices, with delivery you can rely on.
